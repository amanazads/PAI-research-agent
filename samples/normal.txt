Question: Compare three open-source vector databases for a RAG startup

Short answer: For a RAG startup, Chroma is best for early prototyping and small production, Qdrant is the recommended default for lean and mid-scale production due to its operational simplicity and strong filtered search, and Milvus is suited for massive, enterprise-scale workloads exceeding 100 million vectors.

Confidence: high

Key findings:
- Chroma is ideal for prototyping and small production, offering a Python-native experience and a single-container footprint with Apache 2.0 licensing. [S1]
- Qdrant provides efficient single-node deployment, excellent filtered search through integrated payload indexes, and uses Rust for memory-safe execution. [S1, S3]
- Milvus is architected for large-scale enterprise workloads (100M+ vectors) with distributed microservices and Kubernetes-native capabilities, but carries a higher operational overhead. [S1, S3]
